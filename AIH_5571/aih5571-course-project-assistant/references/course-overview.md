## Objectives and intended learning outcomes

The course focuses on the nature, development, and implications of Generative AI; its use in innovative research methods and across disciplines; and its ethical and societal implications.

On completion, students are expected to:

- understand foundational Generative AI concepts (ILO a);
- acquire practical skills using Generative AI technologies (ILO b);
- understand contemporary ethical issues and the effects of Generative AI applications in daily life (ILO c).

## Indicative syllabus

1. Introduction to Generative AI: AI and GenAI basics, history and evolution, deep learning, and large AI models.
2. Applications and impacts: applications, future trends, and AI-assisted research methodology.
3. Tools and technology: popular platforms for ideation, creation, and editing; prompt engineering and iteration.
4. Social good: societal implications and ethical issues such as privacy and AI safety.

These are indicative syllabus headings, not evidence that every topic has already been taught.
